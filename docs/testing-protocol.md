# Testing Protocol — Real-World (Not Conducted)

**Status: not conducted.** This is the original real-user testing protocol,
retained as a ready-to-use reference in case real testing becomes possible
later. The project currently uses a simulated study instead — see
`docs/validation-approach.md` and `docs/simulated-validation-study.md`. No
results in this file are real; the measurement sheet below is intentionally
blank.

## Tester instructions (what to tell a real tester)

> "I'm testing a prototype that estimates how long a phone repair or
> warranty service will take. I'll ask you to enter some job details into a
> simple form and tell me what you think of the result. There's no wrong
> answer — I'm testing the tool, not you."

## Tasks

1. Enter a repair request (pick any service type, phone brand, and job
   details) into the intake form.
2. Review the predicted waiting-time range shown.
3. In your own words, explain what the result is telling you.
4. Decide: would you wait, leave and come back, or do something else?
5. Say whether the information was easy to understand.
6. Give any other feedback.

## Post-task questions (1–5 Likert unless noted)

- Ease of use
- Clarity of the result
- Usefulness of the estimate
- Trust in the estimate
- Willingness to use this if it were real
- Task success (yes/no — did they complete the task without help?)
- Open comment: anything confusing, anything you'd change?
